I'm a fifty-something 'lifestyle singer' with choir experience ranging from children's choirs to university and chamber choirs in adulthood. My voice type is bass. I started studying with Riikka Vuorijärvi about two and a half years ago. Since then, my solo singing hobby has shifted into a completely new gear!

The Alexander Technique that Riikka teaches is based on the natural and holistic use of the body in voice production. Through it, I have discovered an entirely new instrument within myself — Voice with a capital V! Singing has become easier, even though the increased level of demand keeps the challenge alive.

I can warmly recommend Riikka's singing lessons both to those with little experience and to more serious singers. Vocal technique also brings more substance and agility to choir singing.

And by the way: learning voice production and especially proper breathing technique makes everyday life easier for anyone who uses their voice in their work.
