I've studied singing for exactly one lesson — just one — but even that short moment has shaken the way I think about singing.

Riikka's way of teaching is at the same time so simple and natural, yet also wonderfully multi-layered — almost like a philosophy of life. In any case, it has already changed my world.

After the first lesson, people asked me, 'So how did it feel?'

It felt like I had been in music therapy, not at a singing lesson.

We didn't even sing that much, but something opened up inside me. Maybe it was an insight, maybe a connection to the universe, or maybe my soul simply realised its chains had fallen away. My body did try very hard to hold on to its old patterns and tense up in the face of something new. But even so, I was left with a light and good feeling.

So you just have to trust. Life, yourself, and the listener. It's so easy — and so hard.
