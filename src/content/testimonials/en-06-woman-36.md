I came to my first singing lessons feeling excited, but also a bit unsure. For a long time I had dreamed of becoming a better singer and maybe even turning it into a career one day. I hadn't sung very much before, because starting singing lessons felt like a big step.

I'm really grateful that I did. I've learned so much more from Riikka than I ever expected. Through her teaching I've gained confidence, expanded my vocal range, and received help with breath support and singing in tune. I've also learned to connect more with my body.

My dream of becoming a singer no longer feels like just a dream. It's something that is actually moving forward — thanks to Riikka's wonderful teaching.
