I decided to try whether singing lessons might work as a kind of 'exposure therapy' for my stage fright and especially for my breathing-related problems. Luckily, I found Riikka, because she seemed to understand the nature of my difficulties immediately. She has turned out to be an incredibly kind, compassionate, safe and skilled teacher who is not intimidated even by unusual challenges.

Nowadays I manage my work tasks much better, as the fear related to performing is no longer so paralysing. My body also feels more relaxed in general, since my muscles are no longer in a constant state of vague tension. I have also found the connection between body and mind again. In some way, I feel much more present in my own existence than before. And, surprisingly, singing itself feels easier too.

I warmly recommend Riikka's singing lessons to anyone who struggles with performance anxiety.
