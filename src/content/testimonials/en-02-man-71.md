I joined a choir after retiring and attended some singing courses, but I didn't receive sufficient guidance there. I have now been taking weekly lessons with Riikka Vuorijärvi for three years.

Under her guidance, my voice has quickly become freer and I have found resonance in my body. For Riikka, teaching is a holistic process in which both the music and the singer's physical presence are given balanced attention. She applies the Alexander Technique, which is also very useful in everyday life. She is a determined, precise and inspiring teacher.

I admire her musical insight — the character of the song, its rhythm, tempo, tone and message must become clear to the student. In her lessons, it is wonderful to enter into the making of music.
