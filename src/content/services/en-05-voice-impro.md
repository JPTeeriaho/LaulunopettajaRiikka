Voice Impro is a low-threshold, joy-driven safe space where you can gently stretch beyond your comfort zone. If you feel frustrated by technical voice training and performance pressure, Voice Impro offers a refreshing alternative. No singing skills required. No mastery of musical styles needed. Alongside free, spontaneous expression, you'll strengthen trust in your own creativity and the power of authentic self-expression. The most magical moments? When the group begins to resonate as one instrument, intuitively playing in perfect harmony. 
Feel like trying? Join us on Thursday 17th September at 18.30-19.30. Gatherings will take place every other Thursday during the fall 2026.
