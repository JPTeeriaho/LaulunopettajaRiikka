Do you suffer from performing anxiety? Does your speaking voice tire out easily? Would you like a more expressive voice? The same principles underlie both the singing voice and the speaking voice. A more relaxed body produces a more confident and convincing sound. Singers may benefit from doing exercises through speaking, and speakers through singing. Together, we will explore approaches that feel natural to you and help you trust both your voice and stage presence in challenging situations.
