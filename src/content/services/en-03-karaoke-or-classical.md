Would you like to take your singing to the next level? Whatever your genre, we build technique and expression so that your voice carries from heart to heart. I have coached singers in pop, rock, rap, dance music and gospel. My special expertise lies in Baroque vocal style and coloratura technique. During lessons you'll have access to a sound system and microphone. Each year I organize several student concerts where you can perform in a safe and supportive environment.
