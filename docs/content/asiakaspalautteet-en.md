> "As Riikka's student, I have learned how the singing voice is produced naturally and without effort. I had studied singing for several years before, yet every lesson still felt like performing under pressure. Even though I received praise, my voice tired easily, and especially singing the highest notes required force. This often led to a breathy or leaking tone.
>
> When I began studying with Riikka, I felt that I had to relearn the basics of singing. The best part of the new learning approach was that it was no longer based only on imagery or imitation, but on identifying and solving problems using factual knowledge about the body and the respiratory system. For Riikka, every student is unique, and lessons always focus on the individual's needs.
>
> You rarely hear Riikka say 'no' simply because something is too difficult. Instead, you work together to understand why something feels hard and how to solve it in order to reach the best possible result."
> — Woman, 30, doctoral researcher, Oulu

>
> I am extremely grateful that I ended up in Riikka's teaching. In the beginning, we focused purely on producing sound, without pressure to hit the right notes. Nowadays, we work on self-expression, stage presence and the convincing interpretation of my own songs. I also receive feedback on my compositions and lyrics.
>
> The same old blocks sometimes appear again, physically or psychologically. We work through them together — sometimes with physical exercises, sometimes by talking, and by directing attention in a constructive way.
>
> I have found the joy of singing — and even performing. I call my lessons with Riikka 'singing therapy sessions.' After the lessons, I am without exception very energetic and empowered. Thank you, Riikka."
