2. RIIKKA (About page)
   - Hero banner (nature/outdoor image)
   - Two small images beside body text
   - Profile photo of Riikka near the bio text
   - BIO TEXT (English):
     "I graduated as a music pedagogue from Oulu University of Applied Sciences in 2014 and completed a Master of Arts degree at the University of Lapland in 2022. I have worked as a vocal soloist, given my own concerts, and sung in high-level choirs. Teaching has always felt like the most meaningful part of my work.
     My background is in classical singing, but I approach different styles with openness. Rather than shaping voices according to fixed ideals, I aim to help each singer find a way of using their voice that feels authentic and sustainable.
     Questions of vocal wellbeing and expressive freedom are central to my teaching. My interest in these themes began through working with my own vocal challenges. Since then, I have drawn on both research and practical experience to support healthy, nuanced voice use. Many students describe the lessons as strengthening and supportive — sometimes even therapeutic, although they are not therapy."
   - APPROACH SECTION — Alexander Technique:
     "One of the foundations of my teaching is the Alexander Technique — a method for recognizing and reducing unconstructive patterns of use. When the body lets go of excess tension, a more balanced and sustainable vocal technique can emerge naturally. The process of freeing the voice often begins with the breath."
   - APPROACH SECTION — Polyvagal Theory:
     "In my vocal pedagogy I draw from the Polyvagal Theory and trauma sensitive approach. What matters most is creating a space in which both body and mind can feel safe and at ease. When the nervous system is well regulated, healthy vocal technique can emerge more naturally. Singing becomes easier, more enjoyable, and more genuinely expressive."

3. FOR YOU? (Sinulle?) — dropdown menu with 5 paths:
   Hero banner (outdoor/nature image).
   Two small images beside body text.

   PATH 1 — "Varmuutta lauluun ja puheeseen" / "Sing and speak with ease!":
     "Do you suffer from performing anxiety? Does your speaking voice tire out easily? Would you like a more expressive voice? The same principles underlie both the singing voice and the speaking voice. A more relaxed body produces a more confident and convincing sound. Together, we will explore approaches that feel natural to you and help you trust both your voice and stage presence."

   PATH 2 — "Laulutaidoton?" / "Do you feel that singing isn't for you?":
     "Have you been told to stop singing because you were off-key? Do you hesitate to open your mouth because of bad experiences? You're not alone. Singing lessons can be a way to reconnect with your voice at your own pace and rediscover the enjoyment of singing — without pressure or unrealistic expectations. Beginners and advanced singers are equally welcome."

   PATH 3 — "Karaokea vai klassista?" / "Sing karaoke or classical in style!":
     "Would you like to take your singing to the next level? Whatever your genre, we build technique and expression so that your voice carries from heart to heart. Pop, rock, rap, dance music, gospel, Baroque, coloratura. You'll have access to a sound system, microphone, and karaoke backing tracks. Each year I organize student concerts — a safe and supportive environment to challenge yourself."

   PATH 4 — "Hyvinvointia äänestä" / "Experience the wellbeing benefits of singing":
     "Are you longing for a moment to pause and breathe? Singing has well-documented benefits for both mental and physical wellbeing. When the breath becomes freer, it supports the nervous system and brings ease to both body and mind. Lessons can offer a genuine breathing space in the midst of a busy life. Here, you have permission to slow down, to be present, and to explore your voice at your own pace."

   PATH 5 — "Leiki äänelläsi!" / "Voice Impro":
     "Voice Impro is a low-threshold, joy-driven safe space where you can gently stretch beyond your comfort zone. No singing skills required. Alongside free expression, you'll strengthen trust in your own creativity. We explore the full expressive range: vocal textures, spoken word, movement, emotional expression, and drama. The most magical moments? When the group begins to resonate as one instrument, intuitively playing together in perfect harmony."
